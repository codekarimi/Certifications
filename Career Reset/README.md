## 📚 What I Learned

The course teaches the **Career Reset System**, a step-by-step approach to moving into tech with clarity rather than guesswork. Its public framing is three stages: find your path, build your foundation, and get positioned.

### Module 1 — Find Your Path

This stage focuses on choosing a tech direction based on strengths and interests instead of trends. It also addresses the mindset barriers that stall career changers.

**Key Concepts**
- Choosing a role from your strengths, not from what is trending
- Auditing existing skills, experience, and interests
- Recognizing transferable skills from previous roles
- Replacing "I'm behind" thinking with a structured, evidence-based plan
- Defining what a realistic first target role looks like

**Skills Gained**
- I can name a target role and explain why it fits my strengths
- I can list my transferable skills with examples
- I can separate real skill gaps from confidence gaps

**Tools/Frameworks:** Career Reset System frameworks, self-assessment exercises

---

### Module 2 — Build Your Foundation

This stage turns direction into a structured plan with clear next steps.

**Key Concepts**
- Turning a target role into a learning and experience plan
- Breaking a transition into small, ordered action steps
- Choosing projects that build visible proof of ability
- Using structured reflection to keep momentum

**Skills Gained**
- I can write a step-by-step transition plan with priorities
- I can pick projects and certifications that support my target role
- I can track progress and decide on the next move after each milestone

**Tools/Frameworks:** Action-step planning, worksheets, project selection

---

### Module 3 — Get Positioned

This stage covers presenting yourself credibly, even before you are fully trained.

**Key Concepts**
- Structuring a resume around the direction you are heading
- Translating past experience into language a new field understands
- Building a clear career narrative that explains the transition
- Presenting yourself as a credible entry-level candidate

**Skills Gained**
- I can rewrite resume content to highlight relevant, transferable achievements
- I can explain my career change in a concise, confident way
- I can align my resume, profile, and portfolio to one consistent story

**Tools/Frameworks:** Resume positioning, career narrative, portfolio/proof-of-work
